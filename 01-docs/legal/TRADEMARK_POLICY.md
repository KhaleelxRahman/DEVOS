## ❌ PROHIBITED: Trademark Violations

### Category 1: Ownership Misrepresentation

| Violation | Example | Reason | Penalty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Claiming ownership** | "DEVOS by XYZ Corp" | Implies company owns DEVOS | ⚖️ Legal action |
| **Authority claim** | "Official DEVOS Provider" | Implies endorsement | ⚖️ Cease & desist |
| **Inclusion in brand** | "XYZ DEVOS" or "DEVOSify" | Merges brands | ⚖️ Takedown notice |

### Category 2: Trademark Dilution

| Violation | Example | Reason | Penalty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Genericization** | Using "devos" as generic term | Weakens brand value | ⚖️ Enforcement action |
| **Tarnishment** | Marketing harmful content with DEVOS | Damages brand reputation | ⚖️ Legal action |
| **Blurring** | Using DEVOS in unrelated field | Confuses consumers | ⚖️ Opposition filing |

### Category 3: Logo/Visual Misuse

| Violation | Example | Reason | Penalty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Logo modification** | Changing colors, adding elements | Alters brand identity | ⚖️ Takedown notice |
| **Logo removal** | Removing copyright from logo | Violates IP rights | ⚖️ DMCA claim |
| **Unauthorized reproduction** | Using logo in competitor marketing | Brand confusion | ⚖️ Legal action |
